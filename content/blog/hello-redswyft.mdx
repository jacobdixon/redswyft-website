We started Redswyft because we kept hitting the same wall: the tools we relied
on every day kept getting bigger, and the work kept getting slower because of
it. Every quarter brought another sidebar, another setting, another modal asking
us to "complete our profile."

This blog is where we'll write about what we're building, what we got wrong,
and what we learned along the way. No marketing posts disguised as engineering
posts. No "definitive guides" to things we're still figuring out.

## What you can expect

A few things we've decided up front:

- **Short posts.** If we can't say it in a thousand words, we probably haven't
  finished thinking about it.
- **Real numbers.** When we make a claim, we'll show the data behind it.
- **Honest changelogs.** We'll write about what we shipped — *and* what we cut.

If that sounds like your kind of thing, [say hi](/contact). We'd love to hear
what you're working on.
